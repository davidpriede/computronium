Etymology & Foundation

Origin & Conceptual Evolution

01

Charles Stross & Accelerando

The term was popularized by science-fiction master Charles Stross in his seminal 2005 novel Accelerando. Stross explored post-singularity economics where decentralized intelligence transforms everyday objects into supercomputing substrates.

02

Hans Moravec & Pioneers

Earlier speculative robotics and transhumanist work by Hans Moravec laid the groundwork for universal assemblers and crystalline logic matrices capable of maximizing processing speed per gram of matter.

03

Subatomic Rearrangement

Ordinary matter features vast empty space inside atoms and inefficient structural bonds. Computronium reconfigures quarks, electrons, and fields into hyper-dense logic gates where every particle executes parallel operations.
